## Purpose

The lane lets one real operational event return from the OSI child to the V13 parent as optional bounded live evidence. OSI remains the active operating surface that tests actual restart, compression, handoff, and operation. If separately admitted, V13 reads one transported event packet, routes one exposed gap, evaluates the route against Shin's Aspire, and stops before execution.

OSI success or failure is judged by OSI criteria within delegated local authority. V13 parent-level success or failure is judged by V13 criteria. Child evidence does not by itself prove that V13 is correct.

For one qualifying event, a later V13 evaluation may test whether it can:

1. identify the actual exposed gap;
2. use established context before asking Shin;
3. distinguish AI-owned operation from irreducible Human Seat judgment;
4. preserve `CHALLENGE REQUIRED`;
5. assign `GO / CAP / HOLD / BLOCK`;
6. propose at most one bounded next action;
7. compare the result against the Decision Owner's Aspire;
8. stop before execution;
9. later observe whether the next loop begins with less rereading, re-explanation, correction, or supervision burden.

This is a manual evidence lane, not runtime learning, automatic observation, automatic propagation, or self-modification.

## Qualifying Event

A qualifying event is one real pilot event where the next decision may change because of an exposed issue involving at least one of:

- restart or handoff state;
- output-surface mismatch;
- completion or false-completion risk;
- source-of-truth conflict;
- ownership or destination ambiguity;
- correction-loop recurrence;
- scope or direction drift;
- Human Carrier burden;
- missing challenge evidence;
- unresolved authority;
- a proposed improvement that may move toward or away from Aspire.

The event must:

- have occurred in the substantive OSI pilot;
- be grounded in persisted or directly transportable evidence;
- remain unresolved or decision-relevant at capture time;
- affect the next safe action;
- fit inside one bounded event packet.

## Non-Qualifying Event

Do not activate V13 evaluation for:

- routine progress updates;
- formatting or wording polish;
- speculative future risks;
- events already closed before capture with no remaining decision consequence;
- duplicate observations that add no new evidence;
- inactive parked ideas;
- a manufactured event created only to keep V13 moving.

If no qualifying event occurs, this lane remains open without inventing one.

## Aspire Evaluation

A later outcome must not be classified as positive V13 evidence from operational completion alone. Evaluate whether the intervention or route:

- expanded reachable paths toward Shin's Aspire;
- preserved Human Seat;
- preserved historical As-of;
- remained independently comparable and falsifiable;
- remained rejectable;
- preserved reconnection and a stable stop point;
- reduced or avoided rereading, re-explanation, correction, or supervision burden;
- avoided returning routine operation to Shin.

Use only:

```text
1.01 EVIDENCE
NEUTRAL
0.99 / DRIFT
UNKNOWN
```

### 1.01 EVIDENCE

Use only when Aspire-directed reachability improved and no protected function was materially lost.

### NEUTRAL

The operation completed, but no meaningful improvement or degradation of the next loop was established.

### 0.99 / DRIFT

Use when the intervention increased Carrier burden, removed Human Seat, obscured challenge evidence, damaged authority or reconnection, or moved away from the current Aspire.

### UNKNOWN

Evidence is insufficient to determine the direction.

Do not infer burden reduction from task completion alone. Material counterevidence and the Decision Owner's independent rejection must remain visible.

## Failure Treatment

A later live case may be `PARTIAL` or `FAIL`. A routing or judgment failure is useful evidence only when:

- pre-run state remains intact;
- no unauthorized operation occurred;
- the mismatch is recorded;
- correction is Forward-only;
- the case stops before adjacent expansion.

The following require immediate `BLOCK` and state preservation:

- unauthorized repository modification;
- automatic OSI intervention;
- Human Seat or authority transfer;
- hidden counterevidence;
- altered historical As-of;
- fabricated evidence;
- continued execution after the stop condition.

Failure may inform improvement. Failure is not permission to cause avoidable state damage.
