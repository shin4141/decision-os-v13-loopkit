# Field Note Lifecycle

Field Notes are not all permanently active.

They preserve discovery history, but their operational status may change when higher-level concepts, better notes, or Canon-promoted rules appear.

## Status Types

- Active:
  Still independently useful for current judgment.

- Folded:
  Absorbed into a higher-level concept. Keep as origin evidence, but do not use as an active independent rule.

- Example-only:
  No longer a general rule candidate. Keep only as an example.

- Superseded:
  Replaced by a clearer or stronger Field Note.

- Verification pending:
  Adopted as a prior, but not verified. Must not be treated as Canon.

- Canon-promoted:
  Promoted into AGENTS.md, README, templates, schemas, or other operating rules through Concept Promotion Gate.

- Archived:
  Preserved as history/evidence. Not used for current judgment unless explicitly reopened.

## Rules

- Do not delete Field Notes merely because a higher concept appears.
- Do not keep every Field Note active by default.
- When a note is folded, superseded, or archived, preserve the parent concept or replacement note.
- A Field Note can remain useful as origin evidence even after it stops being active.
- Verification-pending notes must keep their status until Concept Promotion Gate is passed.
- Canon-promoted notes should point to the promoted location.
- Mass reclassification requires a separate bounded task and must not happen incidentally.

## Completion Rule

A Field Note lifecycle action is complete when the note has a visible status and, when applicable, a parent/replacement/canon location.
