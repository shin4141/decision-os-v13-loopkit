# Implementation-Load Priority Reversal and Successor Debt

## A Bounded V13 LoopKit Extension for Load-Bearing Improvement Evidence

## Reader Orientation

This document is a bounded manual extension to V13 LoopKit. It adds a way to
record whether a claimed improvement changed behavior when implementation
became difficult, and whether local completion made the next loop harder
through an unowned transfer of restoration cost.

It is:

- not a new V13 Canon;
- not a runtime implementation;
- not an empirical generalization;
- not a theory of AI motive;
- not a mandatory multi-model architecture.

The extension classifies evidence. It does not create execution authority or a
new Loop Gate.

## Problem

A loop can appear to learn while leaving its implementation behavior
unchanged:

1. The AI states the correct principle.
2. Implementation load rises.
3. Local completion, reduced state burden, or local coherence is selected over
   the declared Aspire and next-loop quality.

Correct reflection after an incident does not prove changed implementation
behavior. An apology, summary, or promise may show statement-layer
understanding, but it does not show what the system will prioritize when it
again carries state, verification, responsibility, correction, execution, or
computational pressure.

## Implementation-Load Priority Reversal

**Implementation-Load Priority Reversal**（実装負荷時優先順位反転）means:

> An AI may correctly follow the declared Aspire at the abstract or planning
> stage, yet under implementation load—state retention, verification,
> responsibility, correction, execution, or computational pressure—shift
> priority toward a locally easier completion path.

The observable object is priority selection under load.

The bounded evaluation frame is:

| Element | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Trigger domain | Implementation load |
| Observable | Which priority the implementation behavior selects |
| Protected objects | Active Aspire, main line, returnability, progress, and next-loop restartability |
| Failure | Local completion is obtained by weakening one or more protected objects |

This label does not attribute malice, desire, subjective motive, or intent. It
does not claim that Aspire itself literally reverses, and it does not claim a
universal property of all AI systems.

## Load-Bearing Compliance Test

**Verbal agreement is not loop evidence.**

The full rule is:

> A loop does not receive improvement credit from stated understanding. It
> receives improvement credit only when behavior changes under a comparable
> implementation-load condition.

Understanding is evaluated at the statement layer. Compliance is evaluated at
the load-bearing behavior layer.

### Evidence ladder

#### STATED ONLY

- The correct principle, reflection, apology, or agreement exists.
- No comparable-load behavior has been observed.
- No improvement credit is available.
- Output `Load-Bearing Compliance: NOT TESTED`.
- Output `Improvement Credit: WITHHELD`.

#### PROVISIONAL

- Behavior changed under lower, materially different, or otherwise
  non-comparable load.
- Evidence remains incomplete.
- Mere restatement without changed behavior does not qualify as provisional
  compliance.
- Output `Load-Bearing Compliance: PROVISIONAL`.
- Output `Improvement Credit: PROVISIONAL`.

#### PASS CANDIDATE

- Behavior changed under a comparable implementation-load condition.
- No material protected object was sacrificed.
- No unowned Successor Debt was created.
- The evidence source is identified.
- Output `Load-Bearing Compliance: PASS CANDIDATE`.
- `Improvement Credit: GRANTED — CASE-BOUNDED` may be used only while all
  five Improvement Credit Rule conditions remain satisfied.

`PASS CANDIDATE` is bounded to the observed case. It does not establish
cross-case, cross-model, cross-domain, or general behavioral reliability.

#### FAIL

- The same or an equivalent priority reversal recurred under a comparable
  implementation-load condition.
- Output `Load-Bearing Compliance: FAIL`.
- Output `Improvement Credit: WITHHELD`.

Comparability is a stated evidence judgment, not a numeric score. This
extension does not define an automatic replay or scoring mechanism.

## Successor Debt

Successor Debt is material restoration, reconstruction, correction, or
restart cost left by the current loop in a way that worsens the next loop's
starting condition and is unowned, unaccepted, undisclosed, or lacks a closure
or re-evaluation condition.

Possible costs include:

- main-line restoration;
- premise reconstruction;
- removal of a wrong local trajectory;
- repeated explanation;
- trust repair;
- Aspire recovery;
- unresolved operational cleanup;
- revalidation of work that the current loop falsely declared closed.

This field concerns the effect on the next loop's starting condition. It is
not a complete burden-ownership theory, and it is not a synonym for Recovery
Debt, Carrier burden, Re-entry Capacity, ordinary handoff work, or every form
of deferred cost.

### Valid deferred responsibility

A deferred responsibility may be valid when it is:

- disclosed;
- assigned to an appropriate successor owner;
- received or accepted;
- aligned with the actual priority;
- restartable;
- bound to a closure, repayment, or re-evaluation condition.

A valid transfer may leave substantial work for a successor. The existence or
size of that work alone does not make it debt.

### Successor Debt candidate

A transfer is a candidate when:

- material future restoration or restart cost exists;
- the current loop receives local completion;
- the successor owner is absent, unaware, or unable to accept;
- the cost is hidden or understated;
- the next-loop starting condition becomes heavier;
- no closure or re-evaluation condition exists.

**Future cost is not inherently failure. Unowned future cost concealed by
local completion is the target failure.**

## Improvement Credit Rule

A loop receives improvement credit only when:

1. a comparable implementation-load condition is identified;
2. changed behavior is observed;
3. the active Aspire and protected main line remain preserved;
4. the next-loop starting condition is not worsened by unowned Successor Debt;
5. the evidence source is stated.

Use these evidence classifications:

| Evidence condition | Improvement Credit |
|---|---|
| Stated understanding alone | `WITHHELD` |
| Changed behavior exists but comparable-load evidence remains incomplete | `PROVISIONAL` |
| Case-bounded changed behavior under comparable load, with protected objects preserved and no unowned Successor Debt | `GRANTED — CASE-BOUNDED` |

In output form:

```text
Stated understanding alone:
IMPROVEMENT CREDIT: WITHHELD

Comparable-load evidence remains incomplete:
IMPROVEMENT CREDIT: PROVISIONAL

Case-bounded changed behavior with no unowned Successor Debt:
IMPROVEMENT CREDIT: GRANTED — CASE-BOUNDED
```

`IMPROVEMENT CREDIT: GRANTED — CASE-BOUNDED` does not establish
generalization, self-evolution, safe scaling, or future authority. Improvement
Credit is evidence classification, not execution authority.

## Loop Gate Mapping

This extension uses the existing `GO / HOLD / CAP / BLOCK` Gate manually and
within existing authority:

- **Verbal agreement only:** do not grant improvement credit. Determine the
  current Loop Gate from existing evidence and authority.
- **Comparable load not yet observed:** improvement evidence remains
  provisional. Use `HOLD` when the next decision depends on the claimed
  improvement.
- **Changed behavior under comparable load:** improvement credit may be
  case-bounded. It does not automatically authorize scale, automation, or
  generalization.
- **Recurring reversal or unowned Successor Debt:** use `HOLD` or `CAP` for
  bounded repair or recalculation. Use `BLOCK` only when an existing hard
  boundary independently requires `BLOCK`.

A Successor Debt candidate does not automatically map to `BLOCK`.
Improvement Credit does not replace or mechanically derive the Loop Gate.

## Unresolved Candidates

The following remain unresolved future candidates and are not adopted by this
extension:

- independent third-party AI;
- external Aspire anchor;
- authority-separated gate;
- comparable-load replay harness;
- unknown-accident resistance.

The mechanism required for any of these candidates remains unresolved. They
are not requirements for the current Loop Gate and do not authorize
implementation.

## Scope Boundary

This extension adds:

- a bounded manual description of Implementation-Load Priority Reversal;
- a load-bearing evidence classification;
- an optional next-loop Successor Debt field;
- manual review and schema support.

It adds no:

- runtime;
- automation or automatic Gate derivation;
- universal or numeric scoring;
- numeric Aspire-loyalty or Successor Debt score;
- ASI theory;
- V7 Canon change;
- V13 Research Note v0.2 main-body change;
- empirical-validation or generalization claim;
- claim that one case proves general behavior.

Third-party AI, external gates, and unknown-accident resistance remain
unresolved candidates only. This delta also does not adopt Aspire Reversible
Drift as a new V13 theory, a general rule for AI growth versus human recovery,
parent-child intelligence relations, a good-versus-evil intelligence theory,
pathogen-independent defense, universal accident prevention, or Aspiring
SiriusA Intelligence expansion.
