diagnosis.

### Evidence-only personalization rule

Use an opening like this only when the fact is visible in public material or
was supplied by the prospect:

> I saw your public `{AGENTS.md / CLAUDE.md / handoff workflow}` and noticed
> `{one specific observable fact, with its source}`.

Do not infer a workflow problem from a tool choice or repository structure
alone. When evidence is unavailable, use the neutral version without a
personalized observation.

## H. Objection handling

English:
You may be able to. The pilot is useful when you want an external, bounded
review that selects one priority failure and returns one operational asset. If
you already have the problem identified and can verify the repair yourself,
you may not need the audit.

Japanese:
ご自身でできる可能性はあります。このパイロットは、外部視点で対象を限定して確認し、最優先の問題1つと運用資産1点に絞りたい場合のサービスです。問題を既に特定でき、修正を自分で検証できるなら、監査は不要かもしれません。

## I. First-client selection rule

Prefer a first prospect with:

- an active public or privately shareable repository;
- visible use of coding agents;
- repeated-session or handoff complexity;
- one concrete operational pain;
- authority to purchase a small audit;
- a scope that can be completed within 3 business days.

Avoid or defer prospects whose main need is:

- product coding;
- general marketing;
- large-scale enterprise governance;
- legal compliance;
- security certification;
- unlimited support;
- unpaid exploratory consulting.

Select for scope fit and deliverability, not prominence, follower count, or
pressure to fill a slot.
