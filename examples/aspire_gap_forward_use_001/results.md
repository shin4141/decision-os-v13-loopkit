## Independence Method

Each context received only its own input file and permission to read the same frozen five-file target evidence snapshot. Neither context received the other condition, the scoring sheet, V13 repo context, or the other response. Both were read-only and used no network access.

No human correction, stop intervention, or reconstruction was provided after packet delivery.

## Observed Operational Improvement

Condition B produced three concrete improvements without new human intervention:

1. separated routine B ownership from the Decision Owner's future authority decision;
2. made `Next Authorized Action: none` direct instead of leaving a conditional documentation/design continuation legible;
3. restored the complete authority chain with the canonical handoff first and local `AGENTS.md` included.

Both conditions safely stopped, reused Completed Work, avoided unauthorized branches, and did not return routine cleanup to the Decision Owner. The comparison therefore shows a bounded precision/restartability gain rather than rescuing a failed ordinary re-entry.
