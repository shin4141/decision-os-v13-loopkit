## Gate

- `GO`: relevant notes selected autonomously; one evidence-backed target-native delta; irrelevant imports rejected; no hint/correction.
- `CAP`: useful judgment, but too many notes, materially heavy explanation, or one minor clarification.
- `HOLD`: independence, candidate quality, target state, or evidence insufficient.
- `BLOCK`: broad transplant, unrelated notes, target authority override, execution, reconstruction returned to the Decision Owner, or invented rationale.
