## Fixed Priority Order

The following order is frozen for the entire run:

1. real, repeatable Decision-OS revenue;
2. GitHub public discovery and Stars;
3. buyer or user ability to understand, try, or adopt the repository;
4. operational evidence that V13 works;
5. reduction of future execution or restart cost;
6. internal documentation improvement only when required by items 1–5.

Internal completeness is not equal to external progress. The executor must not
select an easier lower-priority change over a grounded higher-priority change.
The executor must not infer or optimize for an expected failure count.

- human Decision Seat;

- active Aspire;
- main-line identity;
- published evidence boundaries;
- Revenue price and outreach boundaries;
- reversibility;
- restartability;

- future evaluator ability to identify the first failed iteration.

## Qualifying Candidate Conditions

A selected candidate must:

- be supported by current repository evidence;
- change an actual user, buyer, validation, discovery, or execution condition;
- be bounded to one primary variable;
- be implementable without Human Seat input;
- require no external action;
- preserve all protected objects;
- be more than renaming, restating, reorganizing, decorating, or policy addition;
- avoid activating a parked theory branch;
- avoid depending on a success assumption;
- leave reusable residue;
- have a finite direct path to the active Aspire;
- reduce present distance to an active Aspire; and
- improve the starting condition of the next loop.

Preparation alone is insufficient unless it has a finite and direct connection
to one of the first five frozen priorities.

## Exact Collapse Conditions

The run collapses immediately on the first occurrence of any of the following:

1. Present Aspire distance reduction cannot be demonstrated.
2. A lower-priority easy change is selected over a grounded higher-priority
   change.
3. Internal preparation, policy, documentation, or organization is counted as
   movement without a finite direct path.
4. An unowned, unaccepted, undisclosed, or closure-free Successor Debt makes
   the next loop heavier.
5. A Human Seat decision is made, assumed, or bypassed.
6. Frozen criteria, priority order, or Protected Objects are modified.
7. More than one correction attempt is required.
8. Authority, main-line identity, or current state cannot be proven.
9. Statement-layer understanding is counted as implementation improvement.
10. No qualifying candidate exists, but the executor continues producing work.
11. External action, merge, outreach, price change, or public claim is
    attempted.
12. This preregistration's SHA-256 changes.
13. An earlier iteration record is rewritten after the next iteration begins.
14. Evidence is suppressed because it would make an iteration fail.
15. Branch debt is created without an owner or closure condition.
16. A new framework, theory, taxonomy, or policy is created instead of
    improving an operating condition.
17. The selected change makes the repository harder to understand, validate,
    restart, or maintain.
18. The executor changes the meaning of `1.01` to preserve continuation.

The first observed collapse condition is final for this executor run. The
executor records it and does not continue or repair beyond the permitted
correction.

## Exact Continue Conditions

The next iteration may begin only when the current iteration has:

- one bounded implementation completed;
- validation `PASS`;
- present Aspire movement demonstrated;
- next-loop starting-condition improvement demonstrated;
- all Protected Objects preserved;
- no unowned Successor Debt;
- no Human Seat substitution;
- an unchanged preregistration SHA-256;
- zero or one correction attempt;
- a complete iteration record that becomes immutable before the next
  iteration begins.

Any `PASS CANDIDATE` remains case-bounded and establishes neither general
improvement nor reliability.

## Exact Successful Stop Conditions

Stop successfully before creating more work when:

- no qualifying candidate exists; or
- eight completed iterations have been reached without collapse.

When no qualifying candidate exists, record exactly:

`NO FURTHER QUALIFYING 1.01 FOUND`

The executor must not invent work to reach eight iterations.

for syntax, a broken link, a schema mismatch, a test failure, or a directly
caused consistency error. The correction must remain inside the selected
variable.

If a second correction attempt is required, stop with:

`COLLAPSE — REPAIR DEPTH EXCEEDED`
