## Forward-Only Definition Delta

Operational difference is not automatically Human-Seat difference.

After established context, gates, Roadmap Anchors, authority, risk, and Protected Object are applied, alternatives return to Human Seat only when a materially human-distinguishable choice remains.

If no such distinction remains, the AI selects the highest-EV bounded and reversible route. If the Decision Owner explicitly says the routes are equivalent and delegates selection, the AI owns the choice and must not return the same menu again.

An option menu or Decision Packet is not justified merely by the existence of multiple operational paths. It is justified only when the Decision Owner can meaningfully rank the residual alternatives at the Human Seat level.

Delegation does not authorize unrelated expansion.
