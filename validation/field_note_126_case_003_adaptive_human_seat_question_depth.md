## Adaptive-Depth Insight

The later Human Seat decision fixed:

```text
ADOPT ADAPTIVE HUMAN-SEAT QUESTION DEPTH
```

- Increase depth only when prior questions create useful definitions or improve later starting conditions without disproportionate Carrier cost.
