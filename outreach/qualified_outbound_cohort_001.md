## Admission and evidence rules

Every research-pool candidate scores at least 8/10 on the five original dimensions. That score is a research filter, not send authority. A candidate enters the send-ready denominator only when all four non-compensable gates below pass. `Observed` means the public record contains a reproducible trace, log, or bounded before/after result. `Owner-reported` means an affected operator described the incident. `Inferred` means the failure window is demonstrated by source-path analysis but no live occurrence is claimed. A closed issue is evidence of the past operational boundary, not evidence that the defect still exists.

Authority scores distinguish a named or designated buying/partnership function (`2`) from an official commercial or general route with routing authority but no verified technical budget owner (`1`). No personal address, guessed address, commit metadata, issue comment, or private identity source is used.

Before admission, the organizations were compared with repository outreach records and a read-only Gmail Sent search from 2026-06-01 onward. Previously contacted organizations and people—including miryo.AI and every earlier V9/13-13 wave recipient—are excluded. Prior emails and the 2026-07-26 follow-ups are not part of this cohort or its denominator.

## Non-compensable send-readiness gates

1. **Pain Ownership Gate:** the prospect directly owns the observed operational boundary, or its public partner program gives it an explicit commercial reason to add external implementation or verification capacity.
2. **Route-Purpose Gate:** the exact route explicitly accepts vendor, implementation, service-partner, subcontracting, integration-partner, or joint-delivery proposals. Buyer demo, product sales, support, customer-help, and unspecified general-contact routes fail.
3. **Evidence-Boundary Gate:** the public issue supports the stated failure boundary. Where cause is unconfirmed, the advance value is a testable verification invariant, not a claimed root-cause repair.
4. **Economic-Parent Gate:** one economic parent contributes at most one denominator unit unless distinct buying authority and workflow are publicly established.

`PASS` requires all four gates. `HOLD` means a relevant partner route exists but current eligibility or sender fit is not publicly established. `BLOCK` means the verified route has the wrong purpose or a public eligibility rule excludes the current approach. HOLD/BLOCK drafts below remain research records and are not authorized for use.

Public credibility anchor used in every PASS draft: [complete Incident-to-Instruction Before / After example](https://github.com/shin4141/decision-os-v13-loopkit/blob/main/examples/incident-to-instruction-before-after-v0-1.md). It demonstrates the public evidence-boundary method without claiming that the example proves a prospect-specific cause.
