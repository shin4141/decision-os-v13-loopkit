## Observation

PIC was already used as a condition in the V13 Case 004 evaluation surface,
but the local V13 surface did not contain a sufficient PIC definition. Codex
treated that local knowledge boundary as the effective knowledge boundary and
prepared to return PIC definition to Shin as a new Human Seat question.

Shin redirected the route to the existing Decision-OS lineage. Bounded review
of the relevant V6, V7 Addendum, V8, and V11 sources changed the classification
to `LINEAGE-CLOSED / LOCAL-BINDING-REQUIRED`, changed the Decision Route to
`AI-OWNED`, and removed the Human Seat question.

The exact initial draft question and full live exchange do not currently have
a stable repository-bound transcript identity. That part of the incident is
participant evidence, not a verbatim transcript or independent reproduction.
The later source binding, classification, and prevention structures are
repository-verifiable.

## Bounded Claim

In a layered decision system, knowledge may remain preserved in the Decision
lineage while being unavailable in the current local operating surface.

When the AI treats local absence as lineage-wide absence, it may misclassify a
lineage-closed joint as a new Human Seat question. The resulting source search,
layer identification, re-explanation, correction, and routing work is
transferred to the human operator.

In the observed PIC incident, bounded lineage reconnection changed the route
from an unnecessary Human Seat question to an AI-owned local binding. This is
a one-case operational observation. It does not prove that every lineage-
reconnection mechanism reduces burden or that every locally undefined joint is
already answered.

## Failure Mechanism

The route collapsed three distinct states:

```text
Local undefined
≠
Lineage undefined
≠
Human unanswered
```

It also collapsed availability into preservation and source identity into
current use:

```text
Knowledge preservation
≠
current operational availability
```

```text
Source identity
≠
current-layer binding
```

The failure occurred before the Human Seat distinction was safely established.
Local absence was treated as enough evidence to ask, so source discovery and
route correction were shifted to the Decision Owner.

## V11 / V13 / V14 Layer Separation

### V11 — Reconnectability

The PIC knowledge was not necessarily deleted. It was preserved in the
Decision-OS lineage but was not operationally reconnectable from the current
joint without human routing. This is one operational example of preserved
knowledge becoming functionally unavailable.

### V13 — Joint Classification and Routing

The current joint was misclassified because the AI prepared a Human Seat
question before completing a bounded lineage check. V13 later classified PIC
as lineage-closed and made the local binding AI-owned. [Minimum Autonomous Loop
v0.2](../docs/minimum_autonomous_loop_v0_2.md) now requires Decision Lineage
Reconnection before Human Seat return.

### V14 — Human Carrier Burden Attribution

The missing reconnection path did not remove the work. It transferred source
search, explanation, correction, and routing work to the Human Carrier. This
one case does not empirically validate V14 or quantify the transferred burden.

## Human Carrier Burden Transfer

The incident exposed the following Human Carrier work:

- remembering that the answer already existed;
- identifying the relevant Decision-OS layer;
- directing the AI to the correct source family;
- preventing a repeated Human Seat question;
- correcting the route classification;
- supervising the mapping from source definition into current V13 operation.

These burdens arose before the Forward-only prevention structure existed. No
time, token, or cognitive-cost saving is estimated, and no general burden
reduction is claimed.

## Forward-only Prevention Structure

Commit `4ca5e990aea334cf34cbee7193c8cb68308c5962` added MAL v0.2 and the
[Decision Lineage Reconnection Register
v0.1](../docs/decision_lineage_reconnection_register_v0_1.md). The register
binds PIC to verified source identities, keeps source meaning separate from
V13 operational mapping, and records:

```text

Decision Route:
AI-OWNED

Human Seat Question:
none
```

MAL v0.2 requires local, Forward-only-delta, lineage-source, historical As-of,
and source-proof checks before a question may reach the Human Seat.

```text

```

No fresh MAL v0.2 validation, runtime, automatic lineage scan, or automatic
Canon propagation is active.
